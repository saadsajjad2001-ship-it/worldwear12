1) One‑Liner / Core Concept
A fantasy‑inspired, fiction‑fueled fashion platform that blends dropshipping, customization, fandom culture,
and gamification into one immersive experience. Inspired by manhwa, manga, K‑dramas, anime, donghua,
games, and original worlds—customers can wear their favorite stories or even create their own.
2) Vision & Identity
Mission: Awaken human imagination by transforming fantasy into fashion, story, art, and lived
reality.
Identity: The world’s first Idea‑Driven Ecosystem where anyone can create, wear, live, and evolve
fiction—a co‑created infinite world powered by collective thought.
Ultimate Destination: A world where any child’s dream, fan’s fiction, or artist’s idea can be made
real. Burkaya Corp = The Reality Engine.
3) Starting Point — Phase 1: The Fantasy Shop (Zero‑Budget Launch)
Core Product: - Fantasy‑inspired apparel & artifacts (clothing, jewelry, boots, accessories). - Mix of everyday
wearable fashion + cosplay‑level items. - Not exact replicas → inspired reinterpretations (safer IP
stance; unique design DNA).
Execution (Lean): 1) Source from AliExpress / Taobao / 1688 / Temu. 2) Launch a simple Shopify or Etsy
storefront. 3) Use AI tools for brand name, logo, mockups, copy, and visuals. 4) Content engine: TikTok/
Reels series — “Fantasy Fashion IRL?” 5) Collaborate with 1–2 niche micro‑influencers for story‑driven
drops.

Run lore‑driven mini‑drops; A/B test pricing + bundles.
Offer semi‑custom options on 3–5 base designs.
Build waitlist for creator submissions & app beta.
4–12 Months (Community & Systems)
Ship creator submission portal + points system.
Expand to 50–100 SKUs with better QA.

Year 1–3 (Platformization)
Release Imagination Lab web/app.
Scale licensing pilots; first school uniform trial.
Establish factory partnerships; begin limited in‑house production.
Year 3–10 (Global Idea Machine)
AI‑driven co‑creation tools; interactive digital Burkaya World.
Franchiseable uniform programs and global creator royalties.

15) KPIs & Targets (Phase 1)
Audience: 5k email subs; 10k TikTok/IG followers.
Conversion: 1.5–3.0% store CVR; repeat purchase rate ≥ 20%.
AOV: $45–$90 depending on bundles; Gross Margin: 45–60% (improves with in‑house).
Creator Supply: 100+ idea submissions/month; 5–10 ideas selected.
16) Risk & Legal Guardrails
IP: Only inspired reinterpretations unless licensed; avoid direct replicas & protected marks.
Policies: Clear shipping, returns, size guides; QC expectations when dropshipping.
Data: Consent for creator submissions; transparent royalty terms.
17) Lore‑Driven Drop Template
Drop Name: e.g., “Ashen Moon Courier”
Mini‑Story (150–300 words): Sets mood, character, realm.
Pieces: 3–7 SKUs (anchor piece, accessories, optional outerwear).
Unlock Mechanic: Quiz/mission to access limited colorway.
Community Hook: Voting for next chapter or variant.
